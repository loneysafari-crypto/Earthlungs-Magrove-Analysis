Interpreting the Results

This tool maps mangrove health and change across Kisutu I, II & III using Sentinel-2 satellite imagery. Here's a quick guide to what each layer and chart is telling you.

Our Mission

To restore and protect ecosystems, fostering biodiversity and sustainable livelihoods through community-driven restoration and conservation efforts, ensuring a greener, healthier planet for present and future generations.

Our Vision

To be a leader in transforming Africa into a green, sustainable, and climate-resilient continent.

Vegetation Health (NDVI & EVI)

Darker green means denser, healthier vegetation; white, yellow, and brown mean sparse or stressed vegetation. NDVI change shows where things got greener (green) or declined (red) between two dates.

Canopy Density Class

Yellow = sparse canopy, light green = moderate, dark green = dense — a simple three-class summary of the EVI layer above.

Burn Severity

Comparing a date before and after a fire: blue shades mean the area is regrowing, green means unburned, and yellow → orange → dark red means increasing burn severity.

Mangrove Coverage & Change

Dark green marks current mangrove extent. On the change layer: red = lost mangrove, green = stable mangrove, yellow = new/gained mangrove, compared against the 2016–17 baseline.

Restoration Priority

For areas that lost mangrove, this ranks how urgently they need help: red = high priority (little natural recovery), orange = moderate, yellow = low priority (already regrowing on its own).

Reading the Charts

Whenever a run produces a bar chart, it appears in its own panel on the map (top-right, above the legend) using the same colors as the legend — so the chart and the map always agree on what each color means.

Technology

The entire workflow is powered by Google Earth Engine and uses only the COPERNICUS/S2_SR_HARMONIZED collection (Level-2A surface reflectance). Cloud and shadow masking is performed with the Scene Classification Layer (SCL). All map layers, legends, charts, and the interactive analysis panel are generated client-side from the same carefully optimized script, ensuring consistency between static products and on-demand runs.
