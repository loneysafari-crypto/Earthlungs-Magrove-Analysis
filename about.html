About the Mangrove Health Analysis System

The Mangrove Health Analysis System is a geospatial monitoring tool designed to support the assessment of mangrove vegetation health, water extent and environmental changes across selected mangrove restoration sites in Tanzania.

The system uses Sentinel-2 satellite imagery and Google Earth Engine to generate vegetation, water, and burn-related indicators. These results help users understand mangrove conditions, identify areas of degradation and support restoration and conservation planning.

NDVI – Vegetation Health

The Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) measures vegetation greenness and provides an indication of vegetation health and density. Higher NDVI values generally indicate healthier and denser vegetation, while lower values may represent sparse vegetation, bare surfaces, mud or water.

In mangrove ecosystems, NDVI can help identify vegetation extent, assess vegetation condition and monitor changes in mangrove areas over time.

EVI – Enhanced Vegetation Health

The Enhanced Vegetation Index (EVI) is used to evaluate vegetation condition and canopy density. Compared with NDVI, EVI can provide improved sensitivity in areas with dense vegetation and can reduce some effects caused by atmospheric conditions and background reflectance.

For mangrove monitoring, EVI can help distinguish areas of sparse, moderate and dense vegetation and support the assessment of canopy condition.

NBR – Burn and Vegetation Disturbance

The Normalized Burn Ratio (NBR) is used to identify fire-related disturbance and significant changes in vegetation structure. Comparing NBR values before and after an event can help identify areas affected by fire, vegetation loss or other major disturbances.

MNDWI – Water and Land Separation

The Modified Normalized Difference Water Index (MNDWI) helps distinguish water from land surfaces. This is particularly useful in mangrove environments because mangroves are commonly located along coastlines, tidal channels, wetlands and other water-associated environments.

MNDWI can be used together with vegetation indices such as NDVI and EVI to improve the identification of mangrove areas and reduce confusion between vegetation, water, mud and other surfaces.

Burn Severity

The burn severity layer helps visualize areas affected by fire or vegetation disturbance. The colors represent different levels of disturbance and recovery:

  • Blue: Areas showing signs of vegetation recovery.
  • Green: Areas with little or no detected burn disturbance.
  • Yellow: Areas showing moderate disturbance.
  • Orange: Areas showing high disturbance.
  • Dark Red: Areas showing severe vegetation damage.

Technology

The system is powered by Google Earth Engine (GEE) and uses the COPERNICUS/S2_SR_HARMONIZED Sentinel-2 Surface Reflectance dataset (Level-2A).

Cloud and shadow masking is performed using the Scene Classification Layer (SCL). The system processes satellite imagery to generate vegetation, water and disturbance indicators for mangrove monitoring.

The interactive maps, legends, charts and analysis panels are generated from the same processing workflow to maintain consistency between the displayed results and the underlying analysis.

GIS, Remote Sensing and Environmental Monitoring

By integrating GIS, Remote Sensing, satellite imagery, and environmental analysis, this system provides an accessible way to explore mangrove conditions and environmental changes. The results can support field teams, researchers, conservation organizations, restoration practitioners and decision-makers in planning and monitoring mangrove restoration activities.
